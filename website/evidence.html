EdgeFit AI Methodology Evidence status

Evidence status: insufficient evidence

EdgeFit AI has published zero verified global aggregates. This page explains why, and what has to happen before any number appears here.

What exists today

  • A staging backend (Cloudflare Workers + D1) under active test. It is private and not publicly queryable; this page makes no calls to it.
  • Synthetic test fixtures used to qualify the pipeline. They are test-only and excluded from genuine statistics by design.
  • Physical Android measurements collected locally during development. They are single-device, unqualified runs — interesting to the developers, not evidence yet.

What publication requires

  • An approved consent policy (still under review).
  • Genuine device contributions through explicit per-submission consent.
  • At least 5 eligible runs from at least 3 independent installations in the same exact cohort.
  • Individual-run drill-down stays disabled until its disclosure policy is approved. Public viewers will only ever see authorized aggregates.

What you will see when a cohort qualifies

A model table with median throughput, sample and installation counts, and the full cohort identity — one representative cohort per model, never a pooled mean across incompatible runs. Until then: counts without statistics, honestly labeled.
